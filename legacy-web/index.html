The Legend of Mosslantern Glen

High in the misty ridge lies Mosslantern Glen, a secluded valley where damp cliff moss feeds on subterranean crystal dust and glows like little blue lanterns at dusk.

Grandpa Aurelius spent decades tending these fields and charting the shifting caverns beneath the northern cliff. Before retiring, he left you his tools, eight field notes hidden behind farm and dungeon milestones, and the warm company of Aunt Brisa at her seed stall.
